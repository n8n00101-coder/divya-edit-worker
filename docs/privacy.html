Divya bot - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Divya bot is a private WhatsApp assistant used only by its owner. It receives messages, images and videos sent to it by the owner, processes them to reply and to edit videos, and sends the result back on WhatsApp.

We do not sell or share data with third parties for advertising. Message content is sent to AI service providers only to generate replies. Short conversation history is kept for up to 3 days, then deleted automatically.

Data deletion

To delete your data, send "delete my data" to the bot on WhatsApp or email n8n00101@gmail.com. Data is removed within 7 days.
